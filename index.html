About

I'm an Analog Design Engineer at OMNIVISION in Irvine, CA, working on cutting-edge image sensor readout circuits. I hold an MS in Electronic Circuits & Systems from UC San Diego (GPA 3.854) and a BTech in Electronics & Communications Engineering from PES University (GPA 9.1). My interests lie in analog and mixed-signal IC design — from low-noise amplifiers and data converters to PLLs and high-speed SerDes.

This site is where I publish circuit analysis writeups, design projects, and technical notes. Browse the Circuit Analysis and Projects sections to see what I've been working on.
